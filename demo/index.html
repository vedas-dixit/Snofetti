Let it snow on your website

Snow that falls on the page and piles up on your buttons and cards. One line, no dependencies.

See it pile up 
<script defer src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/snofetti"
        data-from="12-01" data-until="01-06"></script>

Drag the window. Shake it. Press the button.

Made for real pages

Scroll, open the dialog, switch the theme. The snow keeps up.

Finds its own ledges

Buttons, cards, images and code blocks collect snow without any setup. Mark elements yourself when you want full control.

Stays out of the way

It never blocks a click, hides under dialogs and menus, and stands still for visitors who ask for less motion.

Knows the calendar

Give it two dates. It turns itself on for the season and off again afterwards, every year.

It snows on everything

Piles further down the page grow while you are not looking.

Three looks

Realistic powder, glassy crystal with coloured glints, or a soft illustrated cap. Switch between them at the top of this page.

Small and alone

No dependencies and nothing to configure. Plain HTML, React, Vue, Svelte and Astro all load it the same way.
