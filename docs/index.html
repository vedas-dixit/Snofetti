Make it snow on any website

Drag a button to your bookmarks bar. Then open any site and click it. The snow piles up on the buttons, cards and images it finds there.

How to use it

  1. Show your bookmarks bar. Press Cmd Shift B on a Mac, or Ctrl Shift B on Windows.
  2. Drag one of the buttons above onto the bar. Each button is a different style. You can add all three.
  3. Open any website and click the bookmark. Click it again to turn the snow off. Click another style to switch.

You can also click a button right here to see that style on this page.

Only your own browser is affected. Reloading a page brings it back to normal.

No bookmarks bar?

Open the browser console on the site and paste this line. It works on many sites, but some, such as YouTube and GitHub, block it. The bookmark buttons work there.

import('https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/snofetti/+esm').then(m => snow = m.snofetti())

Want it on your own site?

One line adds Snofetti to a website for all its visitors. It can turn itself on for the holidays and off again afterwards.

<script defer src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/snofetti"
        data-from="12-01" data-until="01-06"></script>
